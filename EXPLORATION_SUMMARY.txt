FiLM (Feature-wise Linear Modulation):
  • Paper: "FiLM: Visual Reasoning with a General Conditioning Layer"
  • Basic idea: output = (gamma * input) + beta
  • gamma and beta are learned functions of conditioning variables
  • Can handle variable input dimensions and multiple conditions

For missing data handling:
  • Strategy 1 (Masking - recommended): Use identity transform when unknown
  • Strategy 2 (Dropout): Randomly ignore conditions during training
  • Strategy 3 (Imputation): Train separate model to predict missing values
